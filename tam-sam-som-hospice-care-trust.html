Why Now
Four Converging Forces
01 · Private Equity Takeover
Two-thirds of hospice programs are now for-profit — the nonprofit trust foundation is gone
2/3
From 2015 to 2022, 47 private equity firms made 124 acquisitions of US hospices. Today two-thirds of hospice programs operate for-profit — a fundamental reversal from the industry's nonprofit origins. Hospice M&A surged to its highest volume since 2021 in Q4 2025 with 16 transactions vs. just 5 in Q2. Capital consolidation and patient trust move in opposite directions.
PMC12674456 Private Equity in Hospice, 2025 · Hospice News M&A Report, December 2025 · ASPE HHS Issue Brief, January 2025
02 · Fraud Crisis at Scale
$3.4B in suspected fraudulent hospice billing referred to law enforcement in 2025
$3.4B
CMS referred 343 cases to law enforcement for $3.4B in suspected hospice fraud in 2025. As of June 2025, 668 agencies are under enhanced oversight. California's AG dismantled a $267M fraud ring with 21 charged and 119 criminal cases filed. When a dying patient's care is faked for billing, the trust damage to the entire industry is incalculable.
CMS Hospice Oversight Report, 2025 · California AG Press Release, 2026 · Hospice News, January 2026 · HHS OIG Annual Report
03 · Racial Access Gap
Black Medicare patients enroll in hospice at a rate 14 points below White patients
37.4%
Among Medicare decedents, Black beneficiaries access hospice at 37.4% vs. 51.6% for White beneficiaries — a 14 percentage point gap. Medical mistrust, rooted in historical discrimination, is the documented primary driver. 78.2% of eligible families received no introduction to hospice from their physicians. Distrust is structural, not incidental, and the gap is measurable.
Medicare REGARDS Study, 2024 · VITAS Healthcare Disparity Report, October 2024 · PMC10737679, 2024 · Seniors Blue Book Disparity Review
04 · CMS Enforcement Escalation
668 agencies under active oversight — compliance is becoming the price of operating
668
As of June 2025, 668 hospices are subject to CMS medical review under the Provisional Period of Enhanced Oversight, with 122 having billing privileges revoked. CMS has proposed new transparency measures to further strengthen oversight. CAHPS Hospice Survey updated with new quality measures effective April 2025. Regulators are turning trust verification from optional to mandatory.
CMS Hospice PPEO Report, June 2025 · CMS Transparency Measures Press Release, 2025 · AHRQ CAHPS Hospice Survey Update, April 2025
