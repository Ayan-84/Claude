Why Now
Four Converging Forces
01 · Healthcare Trust Collapse
Physician trust fell 44% in four years — and the sickest patients trust least
40%
Trust in physicians and hospitals fell from 72% in April 2020 to just 40% by January 2024. Among Black patients with serious illness, medical mistrust reaches 14.2% — twenty times the rate in White patients (0.7%). The population that needs palliative care most carries the deepest institutional distrust.
Health Affairs / JAMA, January 2024 · Multicenter HCC patient survey, 2018–2023 (PMC12150933)
02 · Chronic Underutilization
Two-thirds of eligible patients die without ever receiving palliative care
67%
Two-thirds of patients with advanced cancer die without receiving palliative care. 55.4% of the public incorrectly believe it is only for the last 6 months of life. Clinicians initiate referrals on average just 2 months before death — years after evidence-based guidelines recommend. Distrust and misconception, not supply, is the dominant barrier.
Newswise / National Cancer Database, 2024 · PLOS One national survey · Palliative & Supportive Care review, 2025
03 · Medicare & CMS Reform Pressure
$28.2B in Medicare hospice payments is under active quality scrutiny
$28.2B
Medicare paid hospice providers $28.2B in 2024 — up 10.1% year-over-year. CMS finalized 2025 hospice payment rules with new CAHPS survey quality measures effective April 2025. For the first time, 53.1% of Medicare decedents received hospice care. Regulatory pressure is converting trust improvement from a differentiator into a compliance imperative.
MedPAC March 2025 Report to Congress · CMS FY 2025 Hospice Final Rule · National Alliance for Care at Home, 2025
04 · AI-Enabled Trust Infrastructure
62% of digital health VC in H1 2025 went to AI-enabled companies
62%
AI in telehealth is growing at a 36.4% CAGR, reaching $6.17B in 2025. In H1 2025, AI-enabled health startups raised an average of $34.4M per round with 11 mega-deals. Telehealth now accounts for 41.2% of the palliative care tech market. The infrastructure to deliver scalable, personalized, trust-building communication at low unit cost is arriving now.
Rock Health Digital Health VC Report, H1 2025 · SNS Insider AI Telehealth Report, 2025 · Market.us Palliative Services Market, 2025
