Framework The 9 Criteria
C1

Infrastructure Layer

Invests in what sits under the application — the measurement layer, trust layer, evaluation layer, assurance stack. Not the app. The foundation the app depends on.

C2

Durable Problems

Backs companies solving problems that predate AI hype and will outlast it. The auto care trust triad is a decades-old structural problem — AI makes it measurable, not invented.

C3

Founder Domain Expertise

Explicitly values founders with rare edge, deep domain proximity, or lived connection to the problem they are solving. Not accelerator founders. Not serial pivots.

C4

Essential Services

Backs companies in industries that shape daily life: healthcare, transportation, financial services, auto care. Markets where trust failures have real downstream consequences.

C5

Inclusion as Embedded Thesis

Expanding access, closing gaps, or rebuilding systems for underserved populations IS the business model — not a DEI overlay applied on top of it.

C6

Multi-Stakeholder Architecture

Invests in AI that must simultaneously serve multiple parties with different interests: consumers, service providers, platforms, and regulators — not single-user AI.

C7

GenAI / Agentic Capability

Actively backs generative AI models, agentic systems, or autonomous AI infrastructure. Understands the specific deployment risks of AI operating with reduced human oversight.

C8

AI for Social Impact

Explicitly directs AI investment toward social good, equity outcomes, or underserved community applications. Not just "responsible AI" — active conviction that AI should serve those typically excluded from its benefits.

C9

Low/No-Code Builders from Exploited Communities

Invests in founders building with accessible, low/no-code tooling from communities historically locked out of technical education and startup capital. Building sophistication is determined by the problem understood — not the tools used.
