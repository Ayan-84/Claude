Investment Thesis Framework
The 6 Threads uMODA Spans
Most AI infrastructure companies speak to 1–2 investor theses. uMODA speaks to six simultaneously — which creates multiple access points into aligned capital and makes the narrative flexible by audience.
Thread 1

Infrastructure Layer

Investing in what gets built under the application — the measurement layer, the trust layer, the evaluation layer. Not the app; the foundation the app depends on.

Thread 2

Durable Problems

Companies solving problems that existed before AI hype and will exist after it. The Problematic Triad in auto care is a decades-old structural problem. AI just made it measurable.

Thread 3

Founder Domain Expertise

Firms that explicitly value founders with a rare edge or deep connection to the problem — not generalist builders applying AI to a market they read about.

Thread 4

Essential Services

Not SaaS optimization. Industries that shape daily life — healthcare, financial services, auto care — where trust failures have real downstream consequences for real people.

Thread 5

Inclusion as Core Thesis

Firms where expanding access and closing gaps for underserved populations is the business model — not a marketing layer on top of it.

Thread 6 · New

Multi-Stakeholder Architecture

AI that must be simultaneously valid and trustworthy to multiple parties with different interests: the consumer, the service provider, the platform, and the regulator. The Problematic Triad made explicit as an investment category.

Why Thread 6 Changes the Pitch

Most AI companies serve one buyer. uMODA serves three simultaneously.

Consumer AI optimizes for the user. Enterprise AI optimizes for the buyer. uMODA's behavioral validity infrastructure must prove trustworthiness to the consumer who is vulnerable to exploitation, the provider who is vulnerable to algorithmic bias, and the platform that loses credibility the moment either party doubts it. That three-sided accountability constraint is what creates the defensible moat — and it's a framing that resonates specifically with marketplace, responsible AI, and network effects investors.
