# Impact-Aligned Investor — Firm Research & Thesis Alignment Analysis

**Date:** May 19, 2026
**Status:** Working Document — Investor Research for uMODA
**Scope:** Identifying VC firms whose thesis elements align with what uMODA is building

---

## What "Alignment" Means Here

Every firm below was evaluated against these five thesis threads:

1. **Infrastructure Layer** — They invest in what gets built *under* the application, not the application itself. The measurement layer, the trust layer, the evaluation layer.
2. **Durable Problems** — They back companies solving problems that existed before AI hype and will exist after it. The triad (in relation to Auto Care) is a decades-old structural problem. AI just made it measurable.
3. **Founder Domain Expertise & Lived Experience** — They explicitly value founders who have a "rare edge" or "deep connection to the problem." uMODA's team building AI validity measurement infrastructure using the same low/no-code tools the methodology is designed to evaluate.
4. **Essential Services & Market Transformation** — Not SaaS optimization. Not incremental. They back companies operating in industries that shape daily life — healthcare, financial services, industrials, auto care. Markets where trust failures have real downstream consequences.
5. **Inclusion as Embedded Thesis** — Not impact labels. Not dual bottom lines. These are firms where expanding access, closing gaps, or rebuilding systems for underserved populations is *the business model* — not a marketing layer on top of it.

### NEW TIER 1 — High Alignment (4–5 Threads)

**ex/ante**
Website: ex-ante.co (Crunchbase profile active)
Thesis: Turning AI risk into opportunity. Invests in tools that explicitly support human agency by increasing privacy, security, and information integrity — capitalizing on rising enterprise and consumer demand for ownership and control.
Stage: Seed stage.
Focus: AI trust infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital infrastructure, human agency systems.

**Why It Aligns:**
- **Thread 1 (Infrastructure Layer):** This is not an application fund. It is explicitly investing in the responsibility stack — the layer that makes AI adoption legally and ethically safe. That is the same layer uMODA occupies.
- **Thread 2 (Durable Problems):** Information integrity and AI trust are structural, pre-hype problems. The demand exists whether or not LLMs are in the news cycle.
- **Thread 5 (Inclusion as Thesis):** "Human agency" as investment criterion maps directly to the situated fairness principle — the right for communities to have AI that actually works for them.

**Ghost note:** ex/ante emerged from the Project Liberty / Reframe Venture ecosystem of responsible AI investors. 91% of surveyed VCs in that ecosystem see major financial opportunity in the "responsibility stack." ex/ante is one of only a handful of funds that has already built its entire thesis around it. They would need zero education on why uMODA's evaluation infrastructure is valuable. The pitch is the market thesis they already hold.

**Threads Matched:** 1, 2, 5 (strong); 3 (implicit)

**Collab Capital**
Website: collab.capital
Thesis: Investing in the building blocks of shared prosperity. Three thematic pillars: Economic Mobility (AI-powered workforce tools, skills gap, capital access), Healthcare Access (tech-enabled care models that close equity gaps), Community Infrastructure (transportation, broadband, housing, food — built for resilience and scale).
Stage: Seed and Series A. $500K–$1M initial checks, up to $2M follow-on. $75M Fund II (June 2025). LPs include Apple and Goldman Sachs.
Founded by: Jewel Burks Solomon + Barry Givens.

**Why It Aligns:**
- **Thread 4 (Essential Services):** Auto care belongs in the same breath as transportation infrastructure. It is not optional technology. Collab's community infrastructure pillar maps auto care directly as a critical services market.
- **Thread 5 (Inclusion as Thesis):** "Proximity is power — founders closest to the problem are best positioned to solve it." This is the same epistemological argument as uMODA's Situated Fairness Principle. They fund from that belief, not as a DEI add-on.
- **Thread 3 (Founder Domain Expertise):** Explicitly backs founders solving real-world problems they have lived proximity to.
- **Thread 2 (Durable Problems):** Economic mobility barriers in auto care — predatory pricing, information asymmetry, trust gaps — predate AI and will outlast this hype cycle.

**Ghost note:** Fund II's explicit AI angle is the "Economic Mobility" pillar — AI-powered tools preparing the workforce for the AI economy. uMODA's evaluation infrastructure could be framed as the validity layer that makes those workforce AI tools trustworthy and auditable. That framing makes uMODA a portfolio complement, not just a portfolio addition.

**Threads Matched:** 2, 3, 4, 5 (all strong)

**Autotech Ventures**
Website: autotechvc.com
Thesis: Solving the world's ground transport challenges with technology. The firm identifies "non-obvious transport market opportunities in niches that require sector expertise." Invests in connectivity, autonomy, shared-use, electrification, and digitization of enterprise — with explicit enthusiasm for "marketplaces, fintech and SaaS" within automotive/mobility.
Stage: Seed through Series C. $1M–$8M initial checks. $500M+ AUM. Fund III: $230M (2023).
Focus: Automotive retail, repair, supply chain efficiency, enabling technologies for electrification and autonomy.

**Why It Aligns:**
- **Thread 4 (Essential Services):** This is the only major VC firm whose entire fund is organized around the automotive sector. Automotive repair and consumer trust in auto services is not adjacent to their thesis — it IS their thesis.
- **Thread 2 (Durable Problems):** Ground transport challenges are structural and decades-old. Autotech explicitly backs companies where the problem exists independent of AI hype.
- **Thread 1 (Infrastructure Layer):** Portfolio includes supply chain visibility, efficiency, and worker safety infrastructure. They back enabling layers, not just consumer apps.

**Ghost note:** "Automotive retail and repair" are explicitly named as investment areas in their fund thesis. The Problematic Triad — information asymmetry between consumer, service provider, and AI intermediary — is precisely the kind of "non-obvious market opportunity in a niche that requires sector expertise" that Autotech is designed to find. They understand why trust failures in auto care have real downstream consequences for consumers and providers alike.

**Threads Matched:** 1, 2, 4 (strong); 3 (partial — sector expertise valued)

**Work-Bench**
Website: work-bench.com
Thesis: NYC-based enterprise AI infrastructure investor. 2026 thesis: "AI is entering an operational phase where infrastructure decisions matter more than model announcements." Backs vertical enterprise software where proprietary workflows and data create defensibility — particularly in regulated industries, supply chain, retail, and complex operational environments.
Stage: Seed stage. NYC-focused but invests nationally.
Focus: Enterprise AI governance and evaluation tooling, data infrastructure, vertical software for regulated industries.

**Why It Aligns:**
- **Thread 1 (Infrastructure Layer):** Work-Bench explicitly identifies "governance and evaluation tooling" as one of the gating factors for enterprise AI adoption. Their 2026 thesis names evaluation infrastructure — not applications — as the constraint on AI deployment in enterprise settings. uMODA's behavioral validity measurement is exactly this infrastructure.
- **Thread 2 (Durable Problems):** Regulated industries (their focus) have compliance and trust problems that predate AI. Those problems don't go away; AI makes them more acute.
- **Thread 4 (Essential Services):** Retail, supply chain, complex operational environments — the categories they back — overlap with automotive services infrastructure.

**Ghost note:** Work-Bench's framing that enterprise AI adoption is now gated by "deployment safety and the ability to plug agents into existing systems without introducing new operational risk" is almost a sentence-for-sentence description of the market uMODA is building the measurement infrastructure for. They don't need to be convinced the market exists. The pitch is: here is the evaluation layer for the enterprise AI deployments you are already funding.

**Threads Matched:** 1, 2, 4 (strong)

### ECOSYSTEM / WARM INTRO LAYER
*Not direct investors. High value as credibility bridges and introduction pathways into their networks.*

**Reframe Venture + Project Liberty Institute**
The leading research and convening ecosystem for responsible AI investment. Their *Responsible AI: The VC Perspective* report (March 2026) surveyed 56 VC investors managing $500B+ in AUM. 91% of surveyed VCs see major financial opportunity in the "responsibility stack." Reframe Venture published the first *Responsible AI Due Diligence Toolkit* for venture investors in December 2025.

**Why it matters for uMODA:** The 56 VCs surveyed represent a warm network of investors already convinced that AI accountability infrastructure is a major financial opportunity. Reframe Venture and Project Liberty are the convening bodies for that network. An introduction into their LP and GP community would surface investors who already hold the thesis — and are looking for companies that match it. uMODA's evaluation infrastructure is the exact type of company their members are actively looking for.
