Framework The 7 Roles Every Entity Plays
R1

Intellectual Legitimacy

Validators whose public alignment changes how every other entity perceives this work. Credibility is contagious. One co-sign from the right voice travels everywhere.

R2

Technical Credibility

Researchers and builders who confirm the methodology is rigorous — that evaluation as infrastructure is a real technical contribution, not a marketing claim.

R3

Policy Translation

Organizations that carry practitioner evidence into governance rooms. Your documented build is the evidence they need. TRAIGA is open. The window is now.

R4

Amplification

Channels, media, podcasts, and communities that carry the story to audiences who don't yet know this work exists. Reach where the argument lands and multiplies.

R5

Pipeline & Capacity

Organizations that create the next generation of practitioners who look like this build. The pipeline flows both ways — they need this methodology; we need their community.

R6

Peer Collaboration

Entities doing structurally adjacent work in different geographies or domains. The relationship is lateral — cross-citation, joint methodology, shared frameworks.

R7

Texas Node

Local ecosystem concentration. What takes two years nationally can happen in six months in person. Houston is the geographic center of Black tech in the US right now.
